Our Story

From a JKUAT lecture hall to 500+ farms.

Vincent Kamau and Anthony Ngotho met as students at JKUAT, immersed in precision agriculture and irrigation engineering. They saw a painful truth:

"Africa doesn't lack farmers. Africa lacks modern farming systems, irrigation access, and practical knowledge."

So they built AgriVolt to fix it. What started as a two-person consultancy is now an East African agribusiness serving over 500 farmers — and growing fast.
