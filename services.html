Services & solutions

End-to-end farming, engineered.

From design to deployment to follow-through — we cover every step a modern farm needs to thrive. Six core service pillars. One unifying philosophy: engineer outcomes, not just installations.

01 · Solar-Powered Irrigation

The AgriVolt wedge.

We lead with what others bolt on as an afterthought. Solar-powered irrigation isn't a feature for us — it's the foundation. Every system we design considers clean energy from day one.

  • → Off-grid solar pumping for boreholes, rivers, and dams
  • → Hybrid solar + grid systems for commercial farms
  • → Solar-irrigation packages for smallholder kits
  • → Energy audits and system sizing
02 · Precision Irrigation

Every drop, every shilling, accounted for.

Drip systems engineered for Kenyan soil, climate, and crops. We design, supply, install, and commission complete irrigation systems with up to 95% water-use efficiency.

  • → Drippers, driplines, surface & sub-surface systems
  • → Sprinkler systems for open-field crops
  • → Fertigation and chemigation systems
  • → Smallholder irrigation kits (0.25–2 acres)
03 · Greenhouse Projects

Turnkey greenhouses, concept to crop.

Full greenhouse delivery — structures, climate control, fertigation systems, and crop planning. We build greenhouses that actually produce, season after season.

  • → Greenhouse structures (polyhouse, glasshouse, nethouse)
  • → Climate control, ventilation, and cooling
  • → Fertigation and integrated water systems
  • → Crop planning for tomato, capsicum, herbs, cucumber, flowers

How we deliver

The AgriVolt 4-phase method.

Every project we touch follows the same rigorous approach. No shortcuts. No surprises.

01

Discover

Farm visit. Water source check. Soil assessment. Crop goals. Budget alignment. We listen first, design second.

02

Design

03

Deploy

Deliver
