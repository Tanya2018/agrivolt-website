Our segments

Built for five distinct kinds of growers.

Each segment has different needs, different budgets, and different success metrics. We tailor our approach for each — but our standards stay the same.

Smallholder Farmers

Affordable solar-powered irrigation kits, drip systems, and farmer training designed for farms under 5 acres. We partner with cooperatives, off-takers, and county programs to bring precision irrigation to where it's needed most.

Commercial Farms

Custom-engineered irrigation and solar systems for horticulture, orchards, cereals, and cash crops. We partner with commercial growers to maximise yield, water efficiency, and ROI — from design through long-term agronomic support.

Greenhouse Growers

Turnkey greenhouse projects — structures, climate control, fertigation, and crop planning. For high-value crops like tomato, capsicum, herbs, cucumber, and cut flowers, we deliver greenhouses that produce season after season.

Government & Donor

Project delivery for national irrigation schemes, county agricultural programs, and donor-funded interventions (World Bank, AfDB, USAID, FCDO, GCF). Tender-ready, compliance-driven, on-time.

Out-Grower Schemes

We work with off-takers, exporters, and cooperatives to deploy precision irrigation at scale across smallholder networks — fueling sustainable supply chains for high-value crops like avocado, French beans, and macadamia.

NGOs & Development Partners

Implementation partner for development projects, climate-smart agriculture initiatives, water stewardship programs, and rural livelihoods interventions. We deliver to donor reporting standards.

Our reach

Active across Kenya and East Africa.

🇰🇪 Central Kenya

Active farmer engagement and project delivery across Kiambu, Murang'a, Nyeri, Kirinyaga, and Embu counties — tea, coffee, dairy, and horticulture belts.

🌅 Rift Valley

Greenhouse projects, large-scale horticulture, and commercial irrigation across Nakuru, Uasin Gishu, Narok, and Bomet — Kenya's most productive agricultural region.

🌾 Eastern & Lower

Smallholder schemes, drought-resilient systems, and solar pumping across Makueni, Machakos, Kitui, and Tharaka-Nithi — where water access changes everything.

🌊 Coast

Greenhouse and commercial horticulture projects across Kilifi, Kwale, and Mombasa hinterlands — high-value export crops with year-round potential.

🦓 Western & Nyanza

Sugarcane, banana, and rice projects across Kakamega, Bungoma, Kisumu, and Migori — where smarter water use unlocks massive yield gains.

🌍 East Africa Beyond

Project engagements expanding regionally — partnerships and assessments underway in Uganda, Tanzania, and Rwanda for cross-border agricultural impact.
