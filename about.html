The AgriVolt story

It started inside a JKUAT lecture hall.

Vincent Kamau and Anthony Ngotho met as students at JKUAT, where they immersed themselves in precision agriculture, irrigation engineering, climate-smart farming, and modern agribusiness systems. Inspired by Kenya's smart-farming research community and a growing wave of African agribusiness innovation, the duo studied the field deeply — and saw a truth too uncomfortable to ignore.

"Africa doesn't lack farmers. Africa lacks modern farming systems, irrigation access, and practical agricultural knowledge."

While their classmates lined up for job applications, Vincent and Anthony went the other way — straight into the field. They visited farms across Central, Rift Valley, and Eastern Kenya. They listened to smallholders explain why their boreholes ran dry mid-season. They watched commercial growers waste water on inefficient systems. They saw greenhouse projects fail because nobody followed up after installation.

So they built AgriVolt to fix it.

What started as a two-person consultancy is now an East African agribusiness venture engaging and supporting over 500 farmers — and growing fast. On the ground, we're known for one thing above all else: we show up, we execute, and we stay.

Our mission

Make precision irrigation accessible to every farmer in East Africa.

From one-acre smallholders to thousand-hectare estates — every farmer deserves modern tools to grow more with less.

Our vision

A continent where every drop earns its keep.

Africa's farmers growing more with less water, less energy, less waste — and earning more from every season.
